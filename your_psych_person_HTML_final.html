A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Therapy is not about
fixing who you are.

It is about creating enough safety and understanding to meet yourself honestly.

I’m Saranya Iyer, a Counselling Psychologist. My work is rooted in empathy, curiosity and collaboration. I believe therapy should feel like a space where your experiences can be heard without judgment and where change happens at a pace that respects you.

Rather than giving you a list of things you “should” do, we work together to understand your thoughts, emotions, relationships and patterns — and find approaches that genuinely fit your life.
